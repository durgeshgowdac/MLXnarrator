# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MLXnarrator to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or opinions about tabs versus spaces.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## What we don't tolerate

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or spam
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email or contact link on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/durgeshgowdac). Reports are handled with discretion.
